# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Demiurge ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our software and services ("Service").

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** Email address, name, and authentication credentials when you create an account.
- **Billing Information:** Payment details are processed by Stripe and are not stored on our servers.
- **Support Communications:** Any information you provide when contacting support.

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data:** Run counts, feature usage, verifier types used, and error rates (anonymized).
- **Device Information:** Operating system, architecture, and app version for download auto-detection and compatibility.
- **License Validation:** Machine fingerprints for license enforcement (hashed, not personally identifiable).

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Source Code:** We do not transmit, store, or access your source code. All code operations run locally on your machine.
- **API Keys:** Your BYOK API keys are stored locally and never sent to our servers.
- **Repository Contents:** File contents, git history, and project structure remain entirely on your device.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
- Process subscriptions and payments
- Enforce license terms and usage limits
- Send transactional communications (welcome emails, billing receipts, trial expiration notices)
- Analyze aggregate usage patterns to improve the product

## 3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:
- **Service Providers:** Clerk (authentication), Stripe (billing), Keygen (licensing) — each governed by their own privacy policies.
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law or to protect our rights.

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. License validation uses signed tokens, and all API communications use HTTPS encryption.

## 5. Data Retention

- Account data is retained while your account is active.
- After account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days.
- Anonymized usage statistics may be retained indefinitely.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your account and data
- Export your account data
- Opt out of anonymous telemetry

## 7. Cookies

Our marketing website uses only essential cookies for authentication (Clerk session cookies). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or through the Service.

## 10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at privacy@demiurge.dev.
